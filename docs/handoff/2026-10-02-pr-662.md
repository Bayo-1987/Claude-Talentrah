### What changed
- A new employer posting closes in 30 days (server-computed); the form preselects "30 days". `job_postings.closing_date_source` (`'default'`, `'chosen'`, or NULL) records where `expires_at` came from; publishing a draft restarts only a `'default'` date, and a legacy row (date, NULL source) counts as chosen and is never reset.
- Short notice warns instead of blocking: under 3 days away shows "This job closes in N days, so you may not get a reminder before it closes" with "Publish anyway" and "Change date"; a chosen date already past still blocks.
- A daily cron (`/api/admin/send-expiry-reminders`, `30 8 * * *`) emails the organisation's owners once per (posting, closing date) when an open employer posting closes within 3 days; the claim is the atomic step and comes before the send. The email carries an Extend link (`/extend-posting/<token>`): GET only reads, a button POSTs; it extends by 30 days once, and the link dies at the closing date it was issued for, so never later than the closing date.
- Eligibility is exactly "the profile exists and the email is non-blank" (no opt-out mechanism applies to a transactional mail; `src/lib/email/recipient-eligibility.ts`, "ACCT-1 extends this").
- Known limitation, follow-up: chosen dates are stored at 23:59:59.999 UTC, so a US employer's "closes 30 Nov" ends at 15:59 Pacific; the scholarship close-instant model (0204) is the pattern to reuse.
