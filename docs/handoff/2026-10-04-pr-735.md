### What changed
`seniorityAlignment` ("above" = the resume's level exceeds the role's, "below" = the role is more senior than the resume) was worded the wrong way round in two places: the Vet summary sentence and the bare "Above"/"Below" cell on the job card and page. One helper, `src/lib/matching/seniority-words.ts`, is now the only place that turns the value into words: "More senior than you" / "More junior than you" / "Match" for the seeker, and "Above the role's level" / "Below the role's level" / "Match" for the employer applicants page (which has its own perspective; the seeker's "you" would be the employer there). A scan fails if any other file spells the phrases or if a new surface can reach them. No scoring change: the stored score and alignment for 30 resume-level x job-level pairs are pinned from main before the fix and identical after. No migration. Files deleted: none.
