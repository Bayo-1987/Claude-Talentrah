### What changed
Owner report, live on talentrah.com/mentorship: with one approved mentor the Pricing section read "sessions currently range from ₦20,000 to ₦20,000".
- **`formatMoneyRange`** (`src/lib/format-money-range.ts`) is the one place a pair of money bounds becomes words: equal bounds read as one amount, different bounds as "from X to Y", one bound as "from X" / "up to X", nothing usable as `null` (the caller leaves the sentence out). It keeps each range's own currency (en-NG `Intl.NumberFormat`: `₦20,000` is one string, `US$90,000`, `€90,000`). A "compact" style keeps the job-card form ("₦X – ₦Y", "From ₦X").
- **Call sites:** the `/mentorship` Pricing paragraph ("Right now, a session costs ₦20,000." for one price; "Right now, sessions cost from ₦15,000 to ₦20,000." for a spread — the noun is said once), its metadata description (", ₦20,000 per session" / ", from ₦X to ₦Y per session"), and `formatSalary` (job cards, job pages, public job rows; output byte-identical to before, equal salary bounds already collapsed). The homepage mentorship card shows a floor ("Sessions from ₦X"), not a range, and is unchanged. No other hand-built money range exists in billing or the Talent Directory (they show single prices).
- **Free-mentor sentence** ("Some mentors offer sessions for free or as volunteers") appears only when an approved, bookable mentor really is free; otherwise the page says "Mentors can choose to offer sessions for free; if one does, that's shown on their profile before you book, never a surprise afterward." In this schema a null `base_price_ngn` **is** how a mentor declares free/volunteer (`0133`, the apply form's "leave blank if free/volunteer", the mentor cards' "Free / volunteer", and `book_mentor_session` all agree), so null and zero both count. Free and unpriced rows never become the range minimum (the query excludes them and the code filters again).
- **Tests (red first):** the helper (14), the whole rendered sentence with a "session at most once" check, the free sentence both ways, null/zero never a bound, and a scan that renders every range with equal bounds in eight currencies and greps `src` for hand-built ranges (naming file and line). Removing the equal-bounds collapse fails 21 tests. `e2e/mentorship-live-pricing.spec.ts` no longer checks the helper against itself: independent expectations, a fixed fixture mentor price whose literal text (₦1,111) is asserted, and a "no ₦X to ₦X" check.

### Tests changed
Only `e2e/mentorship-live-pricing.spec.ts`: it expected "from ₦X a session" and the two bounds; it now expects the phrases above. Nothing was loosened.
