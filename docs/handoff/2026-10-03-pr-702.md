### What changed
`rankFacts` in `src/lib/jobs/ranking.ts` now bands the feed by the tier the badge shows (`getDisplayMatchTier`: Excellent 80+, Good 70-79, Fair 60-69, no tier under 60, unscreened last) instead of "Good = displayed 60-79", which had merged the Fair tier into Good and put a 3-tag 67% Fair above six 79% Good matches on the owner's feed. Evidence, displayed score (with the Recommended freshness penalty) and tag count still order rows inside a tier. No scoring, schema or migration change. Four existing tests across two files changed with the rule (listed, with reasons, in the PR body); new tests include a property test and A2's different-family cap (59) sorting below every tier.

### Follow-ups
- Related finding, recorded because it is easy to rediscover the hard way (details and evidence in the PR body): on 3 Oct stored `match_scores` fell from 3,864 to 2,101 and were rebuilt to 3,250 because #636 (seniority `unknown` instead of `mid`) made the next ingest run change `seniority` on 205 of 372 in-window postings, and trigger 0069 deletes every user's scores for a posting whose `structured_jd` or `seniority` changes. Benign, one-time, but it exposed a standing gap: ingest changes postings about eight times a day (GitHub Actions every 3 hours plus the daily Vercel cron) while the refresh ran once a day, so readers of stored scores (the weekly digest, the `/auto-apply` page, employer ranking) skipped unscored postings for hours. The fix (the refresh at the end of the ingest route, bounded and skippable) is its own PR.
- **Standing warning:** any change to how ingest derives `seniority` or `structured_jd` mass-invalidates stored scores through 0069 on the next ingest run. Plan a refresh straight after such a change and say so in that PR.
- The stale-only rescore of scores computed before A2 (no `explanation.roleFit`) is its own PR, after A2 deploys.
