### What changed
A signed-in person can request deletion of their account, confirm it from a single-use one-hour emailed link, and from then on is hidden from everyone else in the database (Talent Directory, employer applicant views, mentor discovery, the referral leaderboard), receives no email except the deletion's own three, and has Auto-Apply, Pass and Talent Directory renewal stopped, with 30 days to restore. The stored card is cancelled at Paystack before anything is scheduled (nothing is scheduled if that fails; if Paystack succeeds and the database then fails, renewal is switched off in its own write and the person is told). A flagged session is turned away on its next request by the proxy, from `app_metadata.deletion_pending` on the user it already fetched, so with no extra database read and with the refreshed cookies carried onto the redirect. Nothing is deleted or anonymised: the export (PR 2) and the 30-day purge (PR 3, migration 0214) are separate. Full description in the PR body and `docs/account-deletion.md`.

Left alone on purpose: mentors with history (PR 3); the ledger-versus-repo drift ([#683](https://github.com/Bayo-1987/Claude-Talentrah/issues/683)); the preview-only grant drift on three referral functions (its own dry run, waiting for the owner's yes); refunds (on the owner's list for the lawyer; `refund_policy`/`refund_note` exist unused).
