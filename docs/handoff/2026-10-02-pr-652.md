### What changed
`src/lib/seo/job-page-title.ts`: the company is kept, always. A title is trimmed in this order until it fits 70: drop ` | Talentrah` (kept only when the whole title is 65 or less), then the place (the city or `Remote`), then the role, shortened at a word boundary down to a 24-character floor. A title exceeds 70 only when ` at <Company>` plus the place plus a minimal role cannot fit; that class is `overflowsBecauseOfCompany`, a function, so it can be counted. Collisions (a sibling posting would carry the same title) are separated in order: by country, then with the role cut in the middle, then country with the middle cut, and only last the uncut form. Postings identical in every respect keep one title. It follows #634, whose live crawl had 40 titles over 70 and one at 100.
