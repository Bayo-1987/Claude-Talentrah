### What changed
In the `sidebar-left` and `rail-right` skeletons the summary sat directly on the first main section: `renderSummary` got `className: ""` and the first section got `""` instead of `d.sectionTop`, so the first heading touched the summary's last line (about 6.5pt of white, the same as the gap between the summary's own lines). The first main section now gets `d.sectionTop` when a summary is rendered above it, the handling `header-band` already had. With no summary it still starts flush with the sidebar or rail. 13 catalogue templates, on screen and in print; no stored data changes.

It was pre-existing, not introduced by #639 (6.5pt measured before and after it). Measured gap under the summary went from 6.5–8.0pt to 24.5–26.0pt on the 13 affected templates; all 65 templates and demos were measured and the others are unchanged (16.5–34.5pt before and after). The per-template table is in the PR description.

### Test: red, then green
`e2e/resume-print-summary-gap.spec.ts` prints the fixture on every template and demo and fails when the gap under the summary is not more than the summary's own line gap + 1pt. Red on the unfixed code: 13 templates failed. Green after the fix, with `e2e/resume-print-margins.spec.ts` still passing. It needs poppler (`pdftoppm`), which CI's e2e job installs (#639).
