### What changed
A job title and a resume's experience titles are classified into 17 role families; `roleFit` is same / adjacent / different / unknown. The caps are applied to the STORED score in `computeMatchScore` (different -> 59, unknown -> 79) so Auto-Apply, the digest, the proactive alert and employer ranking cannot forget them; baseline tags (project management, agile, scrum, stakeholder management; microsoft office, excel) count as screenable only in the families they are core to. The Role fit cell shows on the job detail page only: the owner's Option B (S3-52) puts one plain line on a job card, so the cell is opt-in in `MatchBreakdown` and only the job page turns it on. No migration. See the PR body for the user-visible effects and the impact figures (read-only against production, 2026-10-02).
