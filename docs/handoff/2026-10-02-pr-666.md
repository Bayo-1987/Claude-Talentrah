### What it changes
`scholarshipCountdown` (`src/lib/scholarships/close-instant.ts`) is the one place the countdown is worded; the card, detail page, landing page, public row and blog embed read it through `scholarshipDeadlineDisplay`, and the three that show a "Deadline:" line render it through `DeadlineLine`. Rows with NO zone use four states on the earliest-zone (UTC+14) timeline: "N days left", "Last day: deadline D, time zone not stated. Apply now.", "Deadline date has passed in some time zones. May already be closed.", closed; no wording says "today". Rows WITH a zone: "N days left" from 24h, "Closes in N hours", "Closes in under an hour". UTC reads "UTC". The alert email states the absolute deadline (`scholarshipDeadlineStatement`), never a relative count. Whether a row is LISTED is unchanged (the 0204 rule, `isScholarshipOpen`); so are the alert selection windows (`scholarshipDaysLeft`).
