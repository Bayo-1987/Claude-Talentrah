### What changed
- `src/lib/jobs/ranking.ts`: Recommended, External and Saved sort by the DISPLAYED band first (Excellent, Good, Fair, then unscreened last), then well-evidenced (3+ screenable tags) before thin (1-2), then the displayed (capped) score with freshness (Recommended only), then tag count. Before, the sort used the RAW score minus a freshness penalty, so a thin match (raw 100, displayed 79 under the thin cap) outranked a well-evidenced 72. Recent and an active search term are untouched.
- `src/lib/auto-apply/listable-pending.ts` (`fetchListablePending`): the one definition of "listable pending" (job open, live score at or above the Auto-Apply threshold, not thin). `/auto-apply` and the `/jobs` banner both use it, so the banner no longer counts rows the page then refuses to list.
- Accepted consequence (owner, 2026-10-02): bands first means an older Excellent outranks a fresher Good inside the 30-day window.
