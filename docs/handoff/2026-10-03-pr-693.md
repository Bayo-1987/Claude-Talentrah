### What changed
`job_postings.company_name` is a copy of `organizations.name` taken when an employer posts, and nothing updated it on a rename: the Growth Marketer posting read "Talentrah Portal" under an organisation renamed to "Talentrah" on 2026-10-02 15:31Z, in its page title, JSON-LD and OG image. Migration 0216 adds `sync_org_name_to_internal_postings()` and an `AFTER UPDATE OF name` trigger on `organizations` (only when the name changes): it sets `company_name` on that organisation's internal postings, any status, where it differs. Nothing else moves (another organisation's postings, external postings, non-name edits, same-name writes). `SECURITY DEFINER`, `search_path = ''`, no execute for anon or authenticated. A separate guarded data fix (`supabase/data-fixes/2026-10-03-company-name-rename-sync.sql`) corrected the one row that was already wrong.

**Checked before applying (owner's two conditions):** every trigger that fires on UPDATE of `job_postings`, read from the live catalogue on production: `job_postings_guard_closing_date_source` and `job_postings_guard_supersession` (BEFORE; they react only to their own columns), `preserve_job_posting_removal` (BEFORE; acts only on a status change out of `removed`), `match_scores_invalidate_on_jd_change` (only when `structured_jd` or `seniority` changes), `match_scores_prune_on_posting_closed` (only on a change to closed). A `company_name`-only update therefore sends no notification, email or job alert (none of the five does, and there is no http/webhook trigger on the table and the table is in no publication), invalidates no match score, and gives the feed and sitemap no freshness signal: `job_postings` has no `updated_at` (only `posted_at`, `last_checked_at`, `created_at`, none written here), and the sitemap's job `lastModified` is `posted_at`.
