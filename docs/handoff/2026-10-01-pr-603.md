### What changed
`scripts/ci-flake-tally.py --mode pool` counts a CI job as a pool failure when the unit job's own log has a failure block of one of four classes: `drained` (`drained the entire pool`), `claim-user-not-found` (`user_not_found` with a `claimFromPool` frame), `claim-retryable` (`AuthRetryableFetchError` with a `claimFromPool` or `createAuthedTestUser` frame) and `resumes-fkey`. First attempts only, one count per job, bucketed `with-fix` / `without-fix` / `unknown` by whether the tested commit contains the fix (GitHub compare API). `--exclude-branch` drops a named PR's runs (used for #602's own red-state runs); `--baseline-lower` is optional. The classifier is pinned by `tests/scripts/ci-flake-tally.test.ts` on verbatim blocks from real failing runs, including a near-miss that must not match.
