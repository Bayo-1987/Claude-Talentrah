Frequently asked

Questions, answered plainly.

Is Talentrah free to use?

Checking your match score and previewing a tailored resume needs no account. Deeper AI actions — full tailoring, applying, interview prep — use credits: you get a free allotment when you create a free account, with more available if you need them.

What's the difference between Farah and a human mentor?

Day-to-day, Farah handles the matching, tailoring, and coaching. When the stakes are highest — negotiating pay, or prepping for a final round — you're handed off to a real mentor who's done it before.

Do I have to pay for a mentor session?

Many mentors offer free or volunteer sessions. Paid sessions are priced by the mentor and paid directly — never through credits.

Can employers post jobs on Talentrah?

Yes — employers can post roles directly, or work with our team on recruitment and staffing through Talentrah's business services.
